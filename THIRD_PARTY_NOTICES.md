## Noto Sans CJK

font files from Debian's `fonts-noto-cjk` package. The package obtains the fonts
from the [Noto CJK project](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk).

Noto Sans CJK is available under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
The complete Debian copyright and license notice
is included in the image at
`/usr/share/doc/fonts-noto-cjk/copyright` and is copied unchanged from the
`fonts-noto-cjk` package.
